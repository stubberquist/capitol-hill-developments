Data: U.S. Energy Information Administration open-data API, fetched live in your browser and cached for six hours. EIA publishes the weekly petroleum status report on Wednesdays at 10:30 a.m. Eastern; spot prices lag by a few business days.

Coverage note: this dashboard tracks the U.S. reserve only. Other IEA members hold public stocks too — Japan, Korea, Germany, Spain and China among them — but none publish a key-free, browser-readable feed the way EIA does. See the README in this folder for what a multi-country build would take.
